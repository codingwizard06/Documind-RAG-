EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - LEAVE AND BENEFITS

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of annual leave per month, totaling 21 days per
calendar year. Leave accrues from the first day of employment but cannot be used until
after the 90-day probationary period, except in cases approved by a manager. Unused
leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the following year; any
balance beyond this is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is required
for absences longer than 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry over between
years and is not paid out upon termination.

3. PARENTAL LEAVE
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
are entitled to 6 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of
the birth or adoption date and can be split into two blocks with manager approval.

4. REMOTE WORK POLICY
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval and role
requirements. Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed every
6 months. Remote employees must maintain core working hours of 10 AM to 3 PM in their
local timezone for meeting overlap.

5. HEALTH AND WELLNESS BENEFITS
The company provides comprehensive medical, dental, and vision insurance starting on the
first day of employment. Employees may also enroll dependents during open enrollment or
within 30 days of a qualifying life event. A wellness stipend of $50 per month is
available for gym memberships, fitness apps, or mental health services.

6. PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year, in June and December. Reviews
assess progress against goals set at the start of the review cycle and inform
compensation adjustments, which typically take effect the following pay cycle.

7. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses must be submitted within 60 days of being incurred, accompanied by an
itemized receipt. Expenses over $500 require pre-approval from a director-level manager.
Reimbursements are processed within 2 pay cycles of approval.

8. TERMINATION AND OFFBOARDING
Employees are required to provide a minimum of 2 weeks' notice for voluntary resignation.
Final pay, including any accrued and unused annual leave, will be issued within 10
business days of the last working day, or sooner where required by local law.
