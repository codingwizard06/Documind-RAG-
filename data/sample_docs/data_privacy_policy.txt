DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY POLICY

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes the standards for the collection, processing, storage, and
protection of personal data handled by the company. It applies to all employees,
contractors, and third-party vendors who process personal data on behalf of the company.

2. SCOPE OF PERSONAL DATA
Personal data includes any information relating to an identified or identifiable
natural person, including but not limited to: names, email addresses, phone numbers,
IP addresses, government identification numbers, financial account details, and
biometric data. Special category data (health records, religious beliefs, political
opinions) requires enhanced protection measures as described in Section 6.

3. DATA COLLECTION PRINCIPLES
Data must be collected lawfully, fairly, and transparently. The company will only
collect data that is adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary for the
stated purpose (data minimization). Consent must be obtained before collecting any
non-essential personal data, and users must be able to withdraw consent at any time
through their account settings.

4. DATA RETENTION
Personal data will not be kept longer than necessary for the purposes for which it
was collected. Standard retention periods are as follows:
- Customer account data: retained for the duration of the active account plus 90 days
  after account closure.
- Transaction records: retained for 7 years to comply with financial regulations.
- Marketing consent records: retained for 3 years from the date of last engagement.
- Support tickets and communications: retained for 2 years.

5. DATA SECURITY MEASURES
All personal data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2
or higher. Access to production databases containing personal data is restricted to
authorized personnel and requires multi-factor authentication. All access is logged
and reviewed quarterly. Any suspected data breach must be reported to the security
team within 4 hours of discovery.

6. SPECIAL CATEGORY DATA
Special category data requires explicit consent, additional encryption layers, and
access restricted to a named list of authorized roles. Special category data may
never be used for automated decision-making without human review.

7. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS
Individuals have the right to access, correct, delete, or port their personal data.
Requests must be fulfilled within 30 days. Individuals also have the right to object
to processing based on legitimate interest and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority.

8. THIRD-PARTY DATA SHARING
Personal data may only be shared with third parties under a signed Data Processing
Agreement (DPA) that ensures equivalent levels of protection. Cross-border transfers
require Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent legal mechanism.

9. BREACH NOTIFICATION
In the event of a data breach affecting personal data, the company will notify
affected individuals and relevant supervisory authorities within 72 hours of becoming
aware of the breach, where required by applicable law.

10. POLICY REVIEW
This policy is reviewed annually by the Data Protection Officer and updated as
necessary to reflect changes in law, technology, or business practices.
